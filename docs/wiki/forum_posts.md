## Forum constraints

- New users can include at most **two links** per post.
- Image uploads to the forum are unreliable, so images go on the wiki page instead.

## Wiki guidance from AJ Quick (summary, 1 November 2026)

- A dedicated wiki page is the right home. AJ created **Ecto-1A LED Sign Boards** at `/wiki/ecto-1a-led-sign-boards` and will move it into place (Equipment → The Ectomobile) once his move tool works.
- **Host files on GBFans.com** where possible. AJ needs to set this up, so the download bundle is sent to him.
- **Host images on GBFans** by uploading them directly to the wiki page (up to 30 per page). A spot in the Reference Gallery may follow; wiki pages can embed a gallery.
- **Credit** either at the specific spot or at the bottom of the page. The page does both: footnotes inline, plus Credits and References sections.
- Wiki pages are living documents: publish and improve.
